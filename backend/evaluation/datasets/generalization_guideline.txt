NORTHWIND FOODS — ADVERTISING STANDARDS Document ID: NWF-AD-07 Version: 2.1
Important: This is a fictional policy created for software evaluation.

1. NUTRITION CLAIMS Advertising must not describe a food as "guilt free". Do not claim that a snack aids weight loss unless a registered dietitian has reviewed the claim. Nutrition labels must be accurate and match the current recipe.

2. ORIGIN CLAIMS Ingredients must not be described as "locally sourced" unless supplier invoices confirm the origin. Do not state that a product is made in a specific country unless customs records confirm it.

3. CHILDREN Advertising must not target children under twelve. Promotions must clearly separate advertising from entertainment content.

4. PRICES AND OFFERS Promotional prices must be available for the full advertised period. Do not advertise a limited offer when the offer is permanent. All delivery charges must be clearly disclosed.

5. ALLERGENS Advertising must not state that a product is "allergen free". Allergen warnings must be displayed on all packaging.

6. REVIEWS Customer reviews must be genuine and must not be purchased. Do not present paid endorsements as independent reviews.

END OF POLICY
