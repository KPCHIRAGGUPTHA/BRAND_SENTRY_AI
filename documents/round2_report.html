Executive Summary
Overview of the operational Round 2 platform, delivered capabilities, and public deployment

Transforming Policy Documents into Deterministic Pre-Publication Safeguards

BrandSentry AI turns approved brand guidelines into a practical pre-publication review step. A user uploads a guideline document (PDF or TXT), enters marketing copy, and receives deterministic verdicts grounded directly in retrieved source passages. The application explicitly distinguishes a claim supported by verified evidence from one contradicted by policy, and from one for which the available sources are insufficient — preventing fabricated approvals.

What Is Working in Production

• Guideline Ingestion Engine

Multipart upload supporting PDF and TXT with PyMuPDF digital extraction, page boundary preservation, and automatic fallback rejection for scanned non-digital PDFs.

• Evidence-Grounded Review

Dense vector retrieval using local ONNX all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings and persistent ChromaDB with character-exact citation substring validation.

• 3-Way Verdict Classification

Conservative, explainable triage: SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE to eliminate hallucinated approvals.

• Deterministic Safeguards

High-risk prohibited phrase screening (e.g. absolute guarantees, unverified superlatives) and conservative handling of missing evidence.

• Semantic Drift Detection

Deterministic analysis across 7 linguistic categories with severity rating (LOW/MED/HIGH), evidence phrase diffing, and conservative compliant rewrites.

• Accessible Production Cloud

Dockerized FastAPI backend and React + TypeScript static frontend deployed independently on Render with automated HTTPS and health check monitoring.

Problem Statement & Product Goal
Market challenges in pre-publication content governance and the BrandSentry AI design principles

The Real-World Governance Challenge

Marketing copy is generated at unprecedented velocity by decentralized internal teams, external creative agencies, and generative AI tools. However, corporate brand guidelines and legal policies are dense, constantly updated, and trapped inside long PDF manuals. As a result:

  • Unsubstantiated and Outdated Claims: Marketing statements exaggerate product capabilities or quote deprecated statistics without citations.
  • Prohibited Guarantees: Copywriters frequently use banned absolute claims (e.g., "100% risk-free", "guaranteed cure", "fastest on earth") that trigger regulatory action.
  • Semantic Drift: Qualified internal facts ("may assist in recovery") become absolute promotional promises ("guarantees complete recovery") through subtle paraphrase.
  • Review Bottlenecks: Human legal and brand review takes days or weeks, creating friction between marketing speed and compliance rigor.
Product Principles Retained from Round 1

1. Evidence Before Explanation

No claim is marked compliant without direct character-exact substring evidence from an uploaded, authorized brand guideline. Unsubstantiated claims must never receive silent approval.

2. Missing Evidence ≠ Falsity

The system strictly separates INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE from CONTRADICTED. If a claim is not addressed in policy, the engine reports missing evidence rather than guessing.

3. Explain and Correct

Beyond issuing a verdict, the system produces actionable explanations and conservative suggested rewrites that preserve promotional tone while eliminating compliance risk.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

BrandSentry AI is designed as reviewer-augmentation software. It exposes full audit evidence, confidence scores, and reasoning to empower human sign-off.

Round 2 Advancement: Semantic Drift Detection
Detecting subtle, dangerous shifts in meaning between internal facts and external marketing claims

Why Vector Similarity Alone Is Insufficient

Standard dense vector retrieval measures topical proximity, not factual precision. A claim can have 95% vector similarity to a guideline passage while reversing its truth value or removing critical legal qualifications. BrandSentry AI introduces a deterministic linguistic drift detector that evaluates claim-evidence pairs across 7 distinct categories.

Drift Category What the Engine Evaluates Illustrative Linguistic Example
CERTAINTY_SHIFT Modality change from possibility or probability to an absolute certainty. "may improve efficiency" → "always improves efficiency"
SCOPE_EXPANSION Broadening audience or eligibility from a qualified cohort to everyone. "registered enterprise users" → "all customers"
NUMERICAL_EXAGGERATION Claimed numerical value, metric, or percentage exceeds evidence figure. "up to 15% faster" → "85% faster"
CONDITION_OMISSION Removing prerequisites, dependencies, or required caveats. "if unused within 30 days" → "within 30 days"
TEMPORAL_SHIFT Altering timing, duration, latency, or frequency parameters. "usually ships within 48h" → "instant dispatch"
CAUSALITY_SHIFT Upgrading an observed correlation into an unproven direct cause. "is associated with lower churn" → "eliminates customer churn"
COMPARISON_SHIFT Transforming a qualified comparative into an unsubstantiated superlative. "competitive performance" → "fastest solution in the world"

What the UI Presents to Reviewers

  • Severity Rating: LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH.
  • Phrase-Level Comparison: Specific claim phrase vs source phrase.
  • Conservative Suggested Rewrite: Restores compliant phrasing while maintaining promotional punch.

Deterministic Guardrails

  • Zero Fabrication: Drift engine never invents evidence citations.
  • Topical Overlap Filter: Rejects false flags when sentences discuss unrelated subjects.
  • Preserves Verdict Semantics: A drift flag supplements, but never blindly overturns, verified evidence.

Changes from Plan & Known Limitations
Intentional architectural scope adaptations and transparent mitigation strategies
Architectural Scope Adaptations Made During Round 2

Vertical Slice Prioritization

Narrowed the MVP scope to claim-level verification against uploaded guidelines rather than building an incomplete multi-role project management suite.

Added Semantic Drift Detection

Recognizing that vector similarity alone misses subtle qualification drops, we designed and shipped a deterministic 7-category drift engine in Round 2.

Simplified Workflow Lifecycle

Replaced multi-stage human approval state machines with an immediate, interactive reviewer dashboard featuring expandable evidence cards and instant diffs.

Pragmatic Cloud Constraints

Used embedded SQLite and local ChromaDB instead of external managed PostgreSQL and Redis clusters, ensuring complete zero-cost portability on Render free tier.

Known System Limitations & Mitigations
Limitation Potential Failure Mode Implemented Safeguard / Mitigation
Heuristic Drift Rules May miss highly nuanced domain idioms or novel indirect paraphrases. UI presents verbatim source passage; flags uncertainty and keeps human reviewer in control.
Vector Retrieval Misses Guideline passage may not appear in top-k if vocabulary differs drastically. Never claims a missing passage means the claim is false; safely returns INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
Ephemeral Free Hosting Render free containers restart on idle, clearing local uploaded documents. One-click sample guideline loader in UI enables instant re-hydration for demo sessions.
Scanned Document PDFs PyMuPDF cannot read text from rasterized image scans without OCR. HTTP 422 diagnostic rejection informs user document contains no extractable digital text.
No Legal Certification AI outputs cannot replace licensed legal or regulatory compliance review. Explicit disclaimers in UI; verdicts formatted strictly as decision-support suggestions.

Develop a standardized benchmark dataset of 150+ labeled claims with ground-truth evidence spans. Eliminates keyword misses for exact regulatory article citations (e.g. "Rule 24.b").
