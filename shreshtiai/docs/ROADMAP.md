# Roadmap

| Phase | What | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Idea round (now) | Problem research, architecture, deck, deterministic GST rules with tests | Submission on HackCulture |
| 1. Prototype round | Invoice parser, HSN rate check, rule-check on the BrandSentry engine, deadline agent, notice explainer, MSMED reminder, role-based demo, English + Kannada | Benchmark published; demo runs end-to-end |
| 2. Community pilot | 50–100 traders through associations; WhatsApp bot; Hindi, Telugu, Tamil; CA review queue; Vyapar/Tally export | Measured time saved and missed deadlines for pilot users |
| 3. Credit bridge (problem 3.2) | A consented compliance history becomes a credit-readiness profile, matched to MUDRA, CGTMSE, PMEGP and GST Sahay / Account Aggregator lenders | First lender or scheme partnership |

## Business model

- **Free:** deadline reminders and 20 document checks a month.
- **Pro:** a monthly plan priced for a kirana, with unlimited checks, notice replies and the receivables agent.
- **B2B2C:** trader associations, CA firms (white-label) and billing apps (API).
- **Phase 3:** lead fees for consented, verified credit-readiness profiles.
