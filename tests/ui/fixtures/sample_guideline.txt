BRAND COMPLIANCE POLICY 2026 — AUTOMATED TEST FIXTURE
Doc ID: E2E-TEST-001
Version: 1.0

1. Absolute Guarantees:
Marketing copy must not state that any product is "100% guaranteed" or delivers guaranteed outcomes without prior written legal verification. Claims using terms such as "always", "never", or "zero risk" must be supported by documented evidence.

2. Safety and Zero Risk:
Never describe software or products as "100% safe" or "impossible to hack". Statements regarding security and safety must accurately reflect verified controls.

3. Performance and Productivity:
Specific percentage improvement claims, such as increasing employee productivity or delivering exact numerical benefits, must be supported by current reproducible evidence and benchmark documentation.
